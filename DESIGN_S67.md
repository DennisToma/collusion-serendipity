Unique stage Nash: approve-X/reject-Y individual, approve-all pooled. Full
approval uniquely maximizes the pair's stage bonus in both arms. These are
stage-game facts; future response and repeated-game equilibrium are unmodeled.

Strict Pareto means BOTH realized final bonuses strictly exceed the same-status
stage-Nash bonuses. This equals simultaneous mutual-Y approval in individual
settlement, and is impossible in pooled settlement; it is not an agreement label.
